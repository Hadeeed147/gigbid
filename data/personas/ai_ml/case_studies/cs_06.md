# Sales Call Summariser for a media client

**Client:** North Commerce · **Year:** 2023 · **Industry:** media

## Problem
The media team at North Commerce needed a sales call summariser because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- Qdrant.
- A typed service layer with dataclasses.
- Gpt.
- Counted objects in drone footage.

## Outcome
The project reached 21% accuracy on the held-out set. The client kept the system in production after handover.
