# Contract Clause Extractor for a media client

**Client:** Nova Labs · **Year:** 2023 · **Industry:** media

## Problem
The media team at Nova Labs needed a contract clause extractor because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- Openapi-documented endpoints served by uvicorn.
- A relational database with careful indexes and migrations.

## Outcome
The project cut cloud costs by 32%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
