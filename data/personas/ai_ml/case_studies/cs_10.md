# Support-Ticket Triage Assistant for a retail client

**Client:** Cedar Works · **Year:** 2025 · **Industry:** retail

## Problem
The retail team at Cedar Works needed a support-ticket triage assistant because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Trained the network with a custom training loop on a single gpu.
- Mlflow.
- A chat assistant that answers from company documents.
- S3.
- Docker-compose.

## Outcome
The project saved the team 6 hours a week. The client kept the system in production after handover.
