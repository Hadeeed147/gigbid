# Churn Prediction Model for a non-profit client

**Client:** Atlas Realty · **Year:** 2020 · **Industry:** non-profit

## Problem
The non-profit team at Atlas Realty needed a churn prediction model because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Fine-tuned a pretrained encoder on the client's labelled tickets.
- Spacy.
- Automated retraining when data drifted.
- A relational database with careful indexes and migrations.

## Outcome
The project reached 43% accuracy on the held-out set. The client kept the system in production after handover.
