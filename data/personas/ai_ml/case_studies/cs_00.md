# Claims Triage Agent for a travel client

**Client:** Bright Learning · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** travel

## Problem
The travel team at Bright Learning needed a claims triage agent because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Pgvector.
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- Row-level locking to stop double bookings.
- Pytorch.
- Tracked every experiment and promoted models through a registry.

## Outcome
The project cut manual work by 63%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
