# Voice-Of-Customer Topic Miner for a real estate client

**Client:** River Logistics · **Year:** 2021 · **Industry:** real estate

## Problem
The real estate team at River Logistics needed a voice-of-customer topic miner because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Automated retraining when data drifted.
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- Aws.
- Counted objects in drone footage.

## Outcome
The project handled 46k requests a day. The client kept the system in production after handover.
