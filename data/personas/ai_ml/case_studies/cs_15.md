# Agentic Research Assistant for a SaaS client

**Client:** River Works · **Year:** 2024 · **Industry:** SaaS

## Problem
The SaaS team at River Works needed a agentic research assistant because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- An orchestrator with retries and backfills for daily batches.
- Moved the workload to amazon's cloud with object storage for raw files.
- Prophet.
- Adapted a 7b model to the client's tone with low-rank adapters.
- Detected defects on the production line from camera frames.

## Outcome
The project cut manual work by 46%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
