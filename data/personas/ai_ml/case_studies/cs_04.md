# Knowledge-Base Copilot for a e-commerce client

**Client:** Atlas Labs · **Year:** 2023 · **Industry:** e-commerce

## Problem
The e-commerce team at Atlas Labs needed a knowledge-base copilot because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Fine-tuned a pretrained encoder on the client's labelled tickets.
- Pgvector.
- Detected defects on the production line from camera frames.

## Outcome
The project cut manual work by 70%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
