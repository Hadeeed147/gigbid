# Lead Qualification Agent for a real estate client

**Client:** Summit Labs · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** real estate

## Problem
The real estate team at Summit Labs needed a lead qualification agent because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Pinecone.
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- A background task queue that retried failed jobs overnight.
- A stateful agent graph with human approval steps.
- Grounded the assistant's answers in retrieved passages with citations.

## Outcome
The project saved the team 20 hours a week. The client kept the system in production after handover.
