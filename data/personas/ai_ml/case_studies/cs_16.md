# Document Q&A Chatbot for a manufacturing client

**Client:** River Media · **Year:** 2023 · **Industry:** manufacturing

## Problem
The manufacturing team at River Media needed a document q&a chatbot because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Chained retrieval and generation steps with a popular orchestration library.
- Wrote the model in a define-by-run deep learning framework.
- Classified support tickets by intent.

## Outcome
The project brought report refresh down to 3 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
