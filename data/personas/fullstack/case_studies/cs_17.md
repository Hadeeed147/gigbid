# Headless Storefront for a retail client

**Client:** Atlas Realty · **Year:** 2025 · **Industry:** retail

## Problem
The retail team at Atlas Realty needed a headless storefront because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Utility-first styling with a consistent design system.
- A single typed query endpoint so the app fetched only what it needed.
- A typed service layer with dataclasses.
- Reusable ui components with client-side state.
- Lambda.

## Outcome
The project cut manual work by 56%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
