# Saas Billing Dashboard for a fintech client

**Client:** Lumen Labs · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** fintech

## Problem
The fintech team at Lumen Labs needed a saas billing dashboard because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Server-rendered pages with file-based routing for seo.
- Orm models and class-based views.
- An event-driven server handling webhooks.

## Outcome
The project handled 44k requests a day. The client kept the system in production after handover.
