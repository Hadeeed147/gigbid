# Travel Itinerary Planner for a media client

**Client:** North Health · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** media

## Problem
The media team at North Health needed a travel itinerary planner because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Live chat with presence indicators.
- Utility-first styling with a consistent design system.
- Subscription billing with webhooks for failed payments.
- Strict typing across front end and back end.
- One codebase shipped to both ios and android.

## Outcome
The project saved the team 19 hours a week. The client kept the system in production after handover.
