# Invoicing Tool for a marketing agency client

**Client:** Summit Learning · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** marketing agency

## Problem
The marketing agency team at Summit Learning needed a invoicing tool because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Strict typing across front end and back end.
- Row-level locking to stop double bookings.
- Wordpress.
- Shipped reproducible images to the client's servers.

## Outcome
The project grew active users to 88k. The client kept the system in production after handover.
