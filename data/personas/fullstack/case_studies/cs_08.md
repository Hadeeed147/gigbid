# Booking Platform for a legal client

**Client:** Blue Learning · **Year:** 2023 · **Industry:** legal

## Problem
The legal team at Blue Learning needed a booking platform because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Subscription billing with webhooks for failed payments.
- Socket.io.
- Redis.
- Strict typing across front end and back end.

## Outcome
The project cut manual work by 33%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
