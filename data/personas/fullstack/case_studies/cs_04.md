# Logistics Tracking Dashboard for a travel client

**Client:** Harbor Finance · **Year:** 2021 · **Industry:** travel

## Problem
The travel team at Harbor Finance needed a logistics tracking dashboard because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Utility-first styling with a consistent design system.
- Postgres.
- Reusable ui components with client-side state.
- Redis.
- Subscription billing with webhooks for failed payments.

## Outcome
The project reduced response time from 21 hours to 17 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
