# Data Quality Monitor for a education client

**Client:** Peak Media · **Year:** 2022 · **Industry:** education

## Problem
The education team at Peak Media needed a data quality monitor because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- A typed service layer with dataclasses.
- Drag-and-drop visual dashboards published for executives.
- Time series forecasting.
- Postgresql.

## Outcome
The project brought report refresh down to 11 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
