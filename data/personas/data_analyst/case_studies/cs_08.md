# Warehouse Modelling Layer for a logistics client

**Client:** Pixel Health · **Year:** 2026 · **Industry:** logistics

## Problem
The logistics team at Pixel Health needed a warehouse modelling layer because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Experimentation.
- Extracted, cleaned and loaded the crm exports.
- Merged and reshaped exports programmatically.
- A typed service layer with dataclasses.
- Version-controlled sql transformation models with tests.

## Outcome
The project cut cloud costs by 62%. The client kept the system in production after handover.
