# Store Demand Forecast for a logistics client

**Client:** Bright Finance · **Year:** 2025 · **Industry:** logistics

## Problem
The logistics team at Bright Finance needed a store demand forecast because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Columnar warehouse queries over billions of rows.
- Pandas.
- An orchestrator with retries and backfills for daily batches.

## Outcome
The project reached 24% accuracy on the held-out set. The client kept the system in production after handover.
