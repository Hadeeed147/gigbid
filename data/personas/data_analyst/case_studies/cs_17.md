# Ops Sla Tracker for a travel client

**Client:** Summit Commerce · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** travel

## Problem
The travel team at Summit Commerce needed a ops sla tracker because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Merged and reshaped exports programmatically.
- Measured lift from a pricing change against a holdout.
- Scikit-learn.
- Row-level locking to stop double bookings.
- Airflow.

## Outcome
The project brought report refresh down to 14 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
