# Cohort Retention Model for a media client

**Client:** Cedar Health · **Year:** 2026 · **Industry:** media

## Problem
The media team at Cedar Health needed a cohort retention model because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Hypothesis tests and confidence intervals for the survey.
- Window functions and ctes over the order tables.
- Measured lift from a pricing change against a holdout.
- A semantic modelling layer so every team used the same metric definitions.

## Outcome
The project saved the team 26 hours a week. The client kept the system in production after handover.
