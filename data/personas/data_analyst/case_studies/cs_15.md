# Loan Portfolio Report for a healthcare client

**Client:** Atlas Media · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** healthcare

## Problem
The healthcare team at Atlas Media needed a loan portfolio report because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Vba.
- Measures for year-over-year growth in the bi model.
- A typed service layer with dataclasses.
- Dbt.
- Pandas.

## Outcome
The project handled 17k requests a day. The client kept the system in production after handover.
