# Inventory Kpi Report for a manufacturing client

**Client:** Bright Logistics · **Year:** 2022 · **Industry:** manufacturing

## Problem
The manufacturing team at Bright Logistics needed a inventory kpi report because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Ga4.
- Merged and reshaped exports programmatically.
- Pivot tables and lookups for the finance team.
- Hypothesis tests and confidence intervals for the survey.

## Outcome
The project reduced response time from 4 hours to 3 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
