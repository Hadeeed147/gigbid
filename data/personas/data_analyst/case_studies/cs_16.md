# Donor Segmentation for a healthcare client

**Client:** Harbor Analytics · **Year:** 2019 · **Industry:** healthcare

## Problem
The healthcare team at Harbor Analytics needed a donor segmentation because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Regression analysis.
- Pivot tables and lookups for the finance team.
- A/b testing.
- A cloud data warehouse with separate compute for analysts.
- Predicted weekly demand per store.

## Outcome
The project brought report refresh down to 18 minutes. The client kept the system in production after handover.
