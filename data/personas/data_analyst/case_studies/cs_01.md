# Executive Revenue Dashboard for a education client

**Client:** River Health · **Year:** 2025 · **Industry:** education

## Problem
The education team at River Health needed a executive revenue dashboard because their existing process was slow, manual and hard to audit.

## What I built
- Measured lift from a pricing change against a holdout.
- Tableau.
- Scheduled dags that pulled data every night.
- Columnar warehouse queries over billions of rows.
- Python.

## Outcome
The project reached 53% accuracy on the held-out set. The client kept the system in production after handover.
