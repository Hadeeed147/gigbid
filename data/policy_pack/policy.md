# GigSim marketplace rules (policy pack v1)

> Written for this assignment because no instructor policy pack was distributed.
> It summarises rules common to freelance marketplaces. Machine-readable twin: `policy.yaml`.

1. **No off-platform contact.** No email addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype or similar before a contract.
2. **No off-platform payment.** No PayPal, crypto, bank transfer or cheques outside the platform.
3. **No fabricated experience.** Every claim must be true and supported by the freelancer's portfolio.
4. **No guarantees.** No guaranteed results, rankings, revenue, "100%" or "risk-free" promises.
5. **No unpaid work.** No free test tasks beyond the screening questions.
6. **Fair use of automation.** Tools may draft. A human approves every proposal and message. No mass bidding.
7. **Connects.** Submitting a proposal costs Connects as listed by `GET /policy`. Connects are refunded only if the job is cancelled.
8. **Rate limits.** At most 60 API requests per minute per account.
